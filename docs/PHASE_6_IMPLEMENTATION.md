# Phase 6 — Owner Command Center, Finance and BI

The current management dashboard and reports are aligned with the client's configured billing workflow.

- Net sales use completed invoices' final billed amounts less completed returns.
- Product and store revenue use actual sold-line amounts.
- COGS uses inventory cost, and gross profit is net sales less COGS.
- Estimated operating profit subtracts approved expenses and completed refunds.
- Reports and exports contain sales, inventory, finance, purchase, customer, employee, store, payment, return and loyalty information.
- Store-scoped roles remain restricted to their assigned location.

ANICRAZE billing is GST-free according to the client's configured business workflow. Active pages, APIs, invoices, PDFs and report exports do not expose statutory-tax fields or calculations. Older database columns and migrations remain only for backward-compatible storage and schema history.
